# Marketing Plan: Cariari.Agency

## 1. Executive Summary

This document outlines the marketing plan for Cariari.Agency, a modern real estate listing platform focused on properties in Costa Rica. Our primary goal is to establish Cariari.Agency as the premier online destination for buying, selling, and renting properties in the region. We will achieve this through a multi-faceted digital marketing strategy that emphasizes Search Engine Optimization (SEO), content marketing, and targeted outreach to both property owners and potential buyers/renters.

## 2. Target Audience

Our target audience is segmented into two main groups:

* **Primary Audience (Property Listers):**
  * Real estate agents, agencies, and brokers operating in Costa Rica.
  * Property owners (locals and expatriates) looking to sell or rent out their properties.
  * Property developers showcasing new projects.

* **Secondary Audience (Property Seekers):**
  * International buyers and investors, particularly from North America and Europe, are interested in Costa Rican real estate for vacation homes, retirement, or investment purposes.
  * Local Costa Rican buyers and renters.
  * Expatriates already living in Costa Rica and looking to move or invest.

## 3. Marketing Goals

### SMART Goals

* **Website Traffic:** Increase organic website traffic by 20% quarter-over-quarter for the first year.
* **Property Listings:** Acquire at least 100 new property listings within the first six months.
* **Lead Generation:** Generate an average of 50 qualified leads (inquiries for properties) per month for our listers within the first year.
* **Brand Awareness:** Become one of the top 5 recognized real estate platforms in Google search results for "Costa Rica real estate" within 18 months.

## 4. Marketing Strategies

### 4.1. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

* **Keyword Strategy:** Target long-tail keywords specific to regions and property types in Costa Rica (e.g., "beachfront property for sale in Guanacaste," "luxury homes in Cariari," "mountain view lots near San José").
* **On-Page SEO:**
  * Ensure every property listing has a unique, keyword-rich title, meta description, and URL.
  * Optimize property images with descriptive alt tags.
  * Leverage the existing dynamically generated `sitemap.xml` for optimal indexing.
* **Local SEO:**
  * Create and optimize a Google My Business profile for Cariari.Agency.
  * Encourage reviews from satisfied clients (both listers and seekers).
* **Technical SEO:**
  * Ensure the website maintains its high performance, especially on mobile devices, as this is a key ranking factor.
  * The site is already built with SvelteKit, which is a good foundation.

### 4.2. Content Marketing

* **Blog:** Create high-quality blog content that provides value to our target audience. Topics could include:
  * "A Complete Guide to Buying Property in Costa Rica as a Foreigner"
  * "Top 10 Up-and-Coming Neighborhoods for Real Estate Investment in Costa Rica"
  * "Understanding Costa Rican Property Taxes and Fees"
  * Interviews with local real estate experts.
* **Social Media:**
  * Showcase stunning property photos and videos on Instagram and Facebook.
  * Use Instagram Reels and Stories to give virtual property tours.
  * Share blog posts and market updates on all platforms.
* **Video Marketing:** Create professional video tours for featured properties. This can be a premium service for listers.

### 4.3. Social Media Marketing

* **Facebook & Instagram:**
  * Run targeted ad campaigns to reach potential buyers based on demographics, interests (e.g., "Costa Rica travel," "real estate investing"), and online behaviors.
  * Use carousel ads to showcase multiple properties in a single post.
* **LinkedIn:**
  * Position Cariari.Agency as a thought leader in the Costa Rican real estate market.
  * Connect with real estate professionals and developers.
  * Share market analysis and industry news.

### 4.4. Email Marketing

* **Newsletter:** Build an email list and send out a weekly or bi-weekly newsletter featuring:
  * Newest listings.
  * "Property of the Week."
  * Recent blog posts and news.
* **Lead Nurturing:** Create automated email sequences for users who sign up or make an inquiry, providing them with more information and keeping them engaged.

### 4.5. Partnerships & Outreach

* **Real Estate Agents:** Offer an introductory free listing period or a discounted package for agents who sign up early.
* **Local Businesses:** Collaborate with lawyers, relocation specialists, and property managers in Costa Rica for cross-promotion.
* **PR:** Reach out to international lifestyle and travel publications that feature Costa Rica.

## 5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* **Website Analytics:**
  * Unique Visitors, Page Views, Bounce Rate (Google Analytics).
  * Traffic sources (Organic, Direct, Social, Referral).
* **Conversion Metrics:**
  * Number of new property listings.
  * Number of user registrations.
  * Number of property inquiries (leads).
  * Conversion rate (e.g., percentage of visitors who make an inquiry).
* **SEO Performance:**
  * Keyword rankings for target terms.
  * Number of backlinks.
  * Domain Authority/Rating.

## 6. Budget

A detailed budget will be formulated based on the prioritization of the strategies above. Initial focus will be on low-cost, high-impact activities like SEO and content creation. A proposed starting monthly budget for paid advertising (Social Media and Google Ads) would be in the range of $500 - $1,500, to be scaled up based on ROI.

## 7. Timeline

* **Phase 1 (Months 1-3):**
  * On-page SEO optimization and content strategy development.
  * Launch blog and publish the first 5 cornerstone articles.
  * Set up and start populating social media channels.
  * Begin outreach to real estate agents.
* **Phase 2 (Months 4-6):**
  * Launch paid advertising campaigns on Facebook and Instagram.
  * Develop and launch an email newsletter.
  * Continue regular content creation (2 blog posts per month).
* **Phase 3 (Months 7-12):**
  * Launch Google Ads campaigns.
  * Analyze KPI data and optimize all campaigns.
  * Explore partnership and PR opportunities.
  * Introduce video marketing services.
